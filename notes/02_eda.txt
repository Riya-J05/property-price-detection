BIVARIATE ANALYSIS

Numerical cols vs price_lakhs
- area_sqft 
    - it has a positive relationship w price_lakhs
    - properties with larger areas generally have higher prices
    - However, a few extreme area values (approximately 15,000–25,000 sq. ft.) do not follow the overall trend and may influence the regression line!!!

- bedrooms 
    - positive increasing relationship w price_lakhs
    - Properties with more bedrooms tend to have higher prices

- bathrooms
    - positive increasing relationship w price_lakhs

- age_years
    - weak negative relationship w price_lakhs
    - prices descrease a bit downward as property age increases

- floor
    - weak neutral relationship
    - a slight positive relationship
    - floor alone does not appear to explain much of the variation in property prices, although it may provide useful information when combined with other features!
    
- total_floors
    - very weak neutral relationship
    - regression line is almost flat

- distance_to_metro_km
    - slighlty negative relationship w price_lakhs
    - However, prices vary considerably at similar distances, and the scatter plot does not show a strong, consistent trend!

- nearby_schools
    - weak positive relationship w price_lakhs
    - The regression line is nearly flat, with at most a slight positive trend!

- monthly_maintenance_inr
    - Relationship affected by extreme values!!
    - Most properties have relatively low maintenance costs, 
    while a few observations have extremely high values (approximately ₹75,000–₹150,000). 
    - These extreme values stretch the plot and strongly influence the fitted regression line, 
    making the overall relationship difficult to interpret reliably!



Categorical cols vs price_lakhs
- property_type
    - Independent houses have the highest mean price, 
    followed by builder floors and apartments.
    - Apartments have a slightly lower median price than builder floors.
    - The distributions overlap considerably, and all three categories 
    contain high-price outliers!

- location
    - Paschim Vihar and Gurugram appear to have the highest mean prices in these plots.
    - Rohini and Uttam Nagar appear relatively less expensive on average.
    
- parking
    - Properties with parking have a slightly higher mean price than properties 
    without parking.
    - Their medians and interquartile ranges are fairly similar!
    - Both groups contain high-price outliers.

- furnishing
    - Fully furnished properties have the highest mean price, then
    Semi-furnished and unfurnished.
    - The boxplots also suggest a generally higher median price for 
    fully furnished properties!

- has_gym
    - Properties with gym have a slightly higher mean price than properties 
    without gym.
    - Their medians and interquartile ranges are fairly similar!
    - Both groups contain high-price outliers.

- has_security
    - Properties with security have a slightly higher mean price than those without security.
    - Their median prices are relatively close, and the distributions overlap considerably.
    - Both groups have high-price outliers.
